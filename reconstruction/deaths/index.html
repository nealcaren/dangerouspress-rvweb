How to read the columns

Killed
Black people killed in the episode (structured code black_killed).
Lynching stage
Completed, attempted or threatened lynching, as coded from the dossier; blank when the episode is not a lynching in any sense (most police and lone-attacker killings).
T&B/NAACP
Tolnay & Beck (1995) rule derived from the coded elements: killed, illegal, three or more killers (size inferred for a mob or party), a justice or tradition pretext, officers not the killers. Probable and possible mark elements the sources leave uncertain.
Broad
Adds killings with no stated pretext or a revenge or political one, officer or posse killings with a lynching character, and one or two killers acting for an organized band.
Evidence
Corroborated: independent reports agree. Single source: one report (reprints count once). Contested: reports disagree on whether it happened or on the core facts. ⚑ marks a case whose two codings disagreed and are awaiting a ruling.
Reports
White-press articles cited in the dossier, reprints included.
MWT
Linked to a Monroe Work Today victim record (monroeworktoday.org, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), our only lynching inventory for 1881. An amber “+ …?” pill lists other victims of the same episode in MWT that our table has not linked (each episode carries one MWT link); a dashed “check” pill means only county and month agree.
